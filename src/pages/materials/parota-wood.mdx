Parota wood is a dense tropical hardwood known for its dramatic grain, strength, and natural warmth.

## Why Parota Is Popular

*   High durability for daily use
*   Naturally resistant to warping
*   Rich contrast between light and dark tones

## How It Compares

Compared to walnut or acacia, parota offers:
*   More pronounced grain movement
*   Slightly softer tone
*   Exceptional performance in large slabs

## Is Parota Right for You?

Parota is ideal if you want:
*   A bold, organic statement
*   A family-friendly hardwood
*   A table that improves with age

## Care Notes

Like all solid wood, parota benefits from stable humidity and periodic oiling. See our [Solid Wood Furniture Care](/care/solid-wood-furniture-care) guide for details.
