Solid wood furniture is durable, but it responds to its environment.

## Daily Care

*   Dust with a soft cloth
*   Wipe spills immediately
*   Avoid harsh cleaners

## Oiling & Maintenance

*   Oil every **3–6 months**
*   Use mineral-based or manufacturer-recommended oils
*   Increase frequency in dry winters

## Humidity Matters

Wood expands and contracts naturally. Aim for **40–45% indoor humidity** year-round.

Learn more in [Humidity and Hardwood Furniture](/care/humidity-and-hardwood).

## Repairs

Minor scratches and surface wear can often be repaired—one of the biggest advantages of solid wood over veneer.
