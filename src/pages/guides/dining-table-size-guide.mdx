The right dining table size balances comfort, flow, and visual proportion.

## Seating Guidelines

*   **4 people:** 48–60 inches
*   **6 people:** 72 inches
*   **8 people:** 84–96 inches
*   **10+ people:** 108 inches or custom

Allow 24–30 inches per person for comfortable seating.

## Clearance Rules

Maintain at least **36 inches of clearance** from table edge to walls or furniture. This allows chairs to pull out and people to move freely.

## Shape Considerations

*   **Rectangular:** best for long rooms and larger groups
*   **Round:** ideal for small spaces and conversation
*   **Live edge:** visually larger—plan extra clearance

## Common Mistakes

*   Ignoring chair depth
*   Forgetting walkways
*   Choosing size before room layout
