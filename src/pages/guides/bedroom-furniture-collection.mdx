## Styling Your Space

When styling a solid wood bedroom, let the material breathe.
1.  **Layer Textures**: Pair the hardness of wood with soft linen bedding, wool rugs, and organic cotton throws.
2.  **Keep it Simple**: Let the wood grain be the art. Avoid over-decorating surfaces.
3.  **Warm Lighting**: Use warm-toned bulbs (2700K) to highlight the rich amber and honey tones of the Acacia and Parota wood.
