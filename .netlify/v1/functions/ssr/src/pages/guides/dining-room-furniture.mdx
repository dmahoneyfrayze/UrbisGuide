## Seating Solutions

Don't forget the chairs. A great dining chair should be comfortable enough for a long dinner party and stylish enough to stand on its own.

*   **Dining Chairs**: Upholstered or wood, designed to complement our table heights perfectly.
*   **Benches**: A live edge bench is a fantastic space-saving solution that showcases more wood grain and adds a communal feel to the table.

---

## Choosing the Right Size

Not sure what size table fits your room?
*   **Allow for Flow**: Leave at least 36-42 inches of clearance between the table edge and the wall or other furniture.
*   **Seating Capacity**: A 6-foot table comfortably seats 6, while an 8-foot table can accommodate 8-10.
*   **Visit our [Sizing Guide](/guides/dining-table-size-guide)** for a detailed breakdown.
